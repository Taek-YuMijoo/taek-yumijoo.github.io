This review appeared in the FOCUS section of the art magazine Art in Culture (February 2025, p. 60). It addresses the solo exhibition of Song Yehwan (b. 1995) at G Gallery, which ran from 8 January to 15 February 2025. The works discussed include The Whirlpool (2025), along with two further works from 2025.

The review considers the digital pixel as a unit of emotion and reads the exhibition in relation to a posthuman internet. Song Yehwan works with media-art installations, and the review places the work within the artist's critique of human alienation in the web environment.

The author was commissioned by Art in Culture to write several texts, and in 2024 was selected through the magazine's emerging-critic support program pitch.
