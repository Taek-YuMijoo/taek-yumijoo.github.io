This review appeared in the FOCUS section of the art magazine Art in Culture (February 2025, p. 60). It addresses the solo exhibition of Song Yehwan (b. 1995) at G Gallery, which ran from 8 January to 15 February 2025. The works discussed include The Whirlpool (2025) and two other works from 2025.

I treat the digital pixel as a unit of emotion and read the exhibition in relation to a posthuman internet. Song Yehwan works with media-art installations, and I place the work within the artist's critique of human alienation in the web environment.

Art in Culture has commissioned several texts from me, and in 2024 I was selected through the magazine's emerging-critic support program pitch.
