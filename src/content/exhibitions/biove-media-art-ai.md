Chandra Chronicles: In Neuroverse (2025-26) is an XR installation by the artist Inhwa Yeom. It was shown in AS _____ AS POSSIBLE, the Hanwha × Anthropic Art Commission at Centre Pompidou Hanwha in Seoul, from October to November 2026. The artwork and its concept are the artist's.

## My role

I worked on the piece as AI researcher with the studio Biove, from October to November 2026. I designed and implemented the AI architecture for the work.
