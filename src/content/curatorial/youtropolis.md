YouTropolis won the media-artwork content open call hosted by KAIST Museum, and the exhibition was developed and presented on the basis of that award. The working title in the original portfolio was provisional.

## Curatorial statement

YouTropolis is an interactive media artwork that matches numerical indicators of Korean YouTube channels, such as subscriber and like counts, with residential population data for Korea's legal administrative districts (beopjeongdong), giving a physical substance to an abstract online space. Inspired by the idea that the value of land is proportional to the number of people who occupy it, the work matches the ranking of Korean YouTube channels by subscribers with the ranking of legal districts by population, pairing each channel one-to-one with a specific region.

Visitors take the controls of an aircraft and become pilots. They fly over this virtually constructed Korean city, sensing the geopolitical positions of YouTube channels and feeling their material character through an immersive experience on three projection surfaces in a wide exhibition space. Flying through a sky where real-world data and virtual-space data have been joined, visitors come to perceive a digital platform, usually understood only through numbers and graphs, as if it were an actual city.
