The question was how research on medical AI has changed over time, and where its emphasis lies. The data are about 15,000 medical AI publications from 2015 to 2023.

I analyzed the corpus with computational text analysis and temporal analysis. The analysis identified a shift toward clinical applications after 2020, with the share of applied topics rising by more than 300%.

The work was done as a research assistant in the Computational Analysis of Technology and Society Lab at KAIST, supervised by Prof. Lanu Kim, and contributed to a KISDI policy report (Kim et al., 2024), published in Korean.
