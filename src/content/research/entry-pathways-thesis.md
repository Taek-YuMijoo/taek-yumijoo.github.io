The full title is "The Power of Entry Pathways: A Study of Professional Comparison Discourse in a Korean Online Community." The thesis asks which professions people compare with one another when they talk about work online, and along which dimensions those comparisons are made.

The data are over 5,000 occupation-comparison posts (2017–2024) collected from a Korean online professional community, analyzed alongside occupational information data. The thesis was written in English for the M.S. in Digital Humanities and Computational Social Sciences at KAIST, under the supervision of Prof. Lanu Kim.

The main finding is that the pathways into occupations, rather than occupational similarity alone, shape which professions are compared and the dimensions along which comparisons occur. I designed the study, collected and analyzed the data, and wrote the thesis.
