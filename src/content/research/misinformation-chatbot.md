This work belonged to the Korea Press Foundation project "AI Use and Inequality: Focus on AI Literacy." The question was how to measure AI literacy in practice, by observing how people respond when an AI system gives them false information rather than by asking them to report their own skills.

I built a chatbot that embeds misinformation as the experimental stimulus. The chatbot was deployed in a study with about 200 participants, and I managed the study data. The project was supervised by Dr. Soeun Yang.
