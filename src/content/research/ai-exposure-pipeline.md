This work was part of the KISDI project "AI Transition and Global Structural Change." The question was how to estimate the potential for AI automation across a very large set of occupational records without labeling each one by hand.

I developed an LLM pipeline enhanced with retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) to score 60,000 occupational records. ILO ISCO-08 exposure estimates (Gmyrek et al., 2025) served as reference data, and occupational similarity informed the scoring. Occupational similarity and score similarity were closely aligned (r = 0.88).

The pipeline reduced a labeling process estimated at one month to two days. I also managed the structured AI exposure estimates and carried out statistical analyses of how exposure varies across occupations. The work was supervised by Dr. Aram Moon.
