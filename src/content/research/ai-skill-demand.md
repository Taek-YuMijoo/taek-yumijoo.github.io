This KISDI project, "AI Skill Demand Analysis Using a Skill Value Chain Framework," asked which skills employers look for in AI-related roles.

I collected 2,563 AI-related job postings and applied structural topic modeling (STM) and word-frequency analysis to identify the skill sets in demand. The project was supervised by Dr. Hyunkyung Lee and Prof. Byungjun Kim, and was presented at the 2024 Research Symposium on ICT-Based Solutions to Social Issues, organized under the Ministry of Science and ICT.
